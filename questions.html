Your sound changes

  • Voice compressed so quiet and loud words come out at a steadier level.
  • Music EQ: the mid-range (500 Hz to 2 kHz) is cut by 9 dB to leave room for the voice.
  • Music level: set 25 dB under the voice's peak. Measured in the mix: about 21.6 dB under the voice.
  • Ducking: the music drops further whenever the narrator speaks and comes back in the gaps.
