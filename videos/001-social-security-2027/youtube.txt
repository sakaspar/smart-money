DESCRIPTION
Social Security changes four numbers for 2027: the 3.6% raise, the earnings limit for people who work while collecting, the tax ceiling for higher earners, and the amount it takes to earn a work credit.
This video goes through each one with the official figures, shows who it affects, and ends with a short checklist of what to look at before January.
